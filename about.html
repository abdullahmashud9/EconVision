Our Story & Ethos

About EconVision

A community founded by students, researchers, and professors to make economic discussions, research feedback, and academic mentorship accessible to all.

Why We Started

Bridging Classroom Theory with Real Economic Research

In most colleges and universities, economics is taught through standard textbooks and problem sets. But the real work of economics happens when you start asking hard questions: How do we measure the impact of a welfare policy? Why are inflation forecasts missing the mark? What data actually proves a causal relationship?

For undergraduates and early-career researchers, finding answers to these questions—and getting feedback from experienced economists—can be difficult. Academic journals are dense, conferences are expensive, and professors often have limited office hours.

EconVision was created to change that. We started as a modest group of students and scholars who wanted a collaborative space to read working papers, exchange datasets, and talk through econometrics problems without institutional barriers.

"Economics shouldn't just be memorized for exams. It should be examined, debated, and applied to real-world challenges."
What We Do Daily

Inside the Community

  • 📊 Paper & Preprint Discussions Breaking down recent empirical papers in macro, labor, development, and behavioral economics.
  • 📝 Senior Thesis & Draft Reviews Constructive peer review on methodology, econometric model choices, and data interpretation.
  • 🎓 Grad School & Career Guidance Honest advice on Master's/PhD applications, Pre-Doc positions, RA opportunities, and analytical roles.
  • 💻 Toolkits & Coding Help Sharing scripts, tips, and troubleshooting in R, Stata, Python, and causal econometrics.
Who Belongs Here

Open to Every Curious Mind

Whether you are an undergraduate working on your first regression, a doctoral student preparing a thesis defense, or a professor looking to mentor the next cohort—EconVision is built for you.

How We Operate

The Values That Guide Us

We keep our community focused, respectful, and genuinely helpful.

Core Value 1

Curiosity First

No question is too simple. We encourage students to ask about concepts they find confusing and scholars to explain ideas clearly without condescension.
Core Value 2

Respectful Debate

Economics thrives on disagreement. We welcome diverse schools of thought, provided arguments are grounded in evidence and communicated civilly.
Core Value 3

Zero Gatekeeping

Knowledge and opportunities should not depend on university prestige. We share resources, datasets, and fellowship advice openly with every member.
Core Value 4

High Signal, No Spam

Our moderated community spaces maintain a strict policy against self-promotional spam, ads, and unverified claims to protect members' time.
