The EconVision Community

A diverse, multi-tiered network bridging undergraduate scholars, tenured faculty, central bankers, and policy researchers worldwide.

Membership Cohorts

Where Do You Fit in EconVision?

Our community is structured around four primary participant cohorts, ensuring tailored discourse, relevant opportunities, and multi-directional mentorship.

Cohort 01

Economics Students (Undergraduate & Masters)

Aspiring economists navigating core micro, macro, and econometrics coursework, preparing for graduate school admissions, and writing senior theses.

  • ✓ Peer-led reading groups and econometrics workshops (R, Stata, Python)
  • ✓ Graduate school application tips and statement of purpose reviews
  • ✓ Early-stage thesis feedback from professors and PhD researchers
Active in LinkedIn Discussions
Cohort 02

PhD Candidates & Academic Faculty

Doctoral researchers, postdocs, and university professors dedicated to advancing theoretical and applied economics across global institutions.

  • ✓ Pre-print dissemination and peer feedback before journal submissions
  • ✓ Cross-university research co-authorships and symposium speaking
  • ✓ Recruitment channels for Research Assistant (RA) and Postdoc positions
Faculty & Research Hub
Cohort 03

Policy Researchers & Think Tank Analysts

Economists working within government agencies, multilateral development organizations (IMF, World Bank), and independent policy institutes.

  • ✓ Real-world policy impact assessments and regulatory briefs
  • ✓ Data-driven discussions on fiscal policy, trade, and climate economics
  • ✓ Translation of complex mathematical models into public discourse
Public Policy Forum
Cohort 04

Finance, Banking & Market Enthusiasts

Quantitative analysts, central bank watchers, asset managers, and financial economists analyzing interest rates, inflation, and capital flows.

  • ✓ Central bank meeting previews and interest rate forecasts
  • ✓ Applied financial econometrics and risk modeling exchange
  • ✓ Industry perspective on macroeconomic cycles and policy shifts
Markets & Macro Forum
Community Standards

The EconVision Code of Academic Conduct

To preserve the intellectual standard of our LinkedIn group and community channels, all participants are expected to adhere to our core standards:

  1. Substantive Dialogue: Focus on arguments, methodologies, and data rather than partisan commentary or personal opinions.
  2. Citation Integrity: Attribute sources, pre-prints, and datasets properly when sharing claims or research summaries.
  3. Constructive Critique: Deliver critiques of working papers and theses constructively, aiming to assist author improvement.
  4. Inclusivity & Mentorship: Maintain an encouraging posture toward junior scholars and undergraduate students asking foundational questions.
