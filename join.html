Frequently Asked Questions

Membership Questions

Is membership in EconVision completely free?

Yes. EconVision is an independent academic community. All LinkedIn group discussions, resource repositories, and standard virtual seminars are 100% free of charge.

Who is eligible to join?

Undergraduate and graduate students majoring in economics or related quantitative disciplines, university professors, post-doctoral fellows, central bank staff, policy analysts, and finance professionals with an interest in academic economics.

How are discussions moderated?

Our moderation committee upholds rigorous standards of academic civility, empirical integrity, and constructive discourse. Spam, commercial promotions, and partisan hostility are promptly removed.
