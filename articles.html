Submitting an Insight or Paper Summary

EconVision accepts original research summaries, literature syntheses, policy briefs, and econometric tutorials from students, professors, and professional economists.

Submission Criteria:

  • Clear empirical basis or sound theoretical modeling.
  • Accessible exposition suitable for both specialized researchers and economics undergraduates.
  • Adherence to standard citation formats (APA / Chicago / AEA).
  • Approximate length: 1,200 to 2,800 words.
